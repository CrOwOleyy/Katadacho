# Code of Conduct

Katadacho is a small project, and we want it to stay a friendly place to contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to the tools we use.
- Give constructive feedback about code and ideas, never about people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a change, and explain why when they do.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a private security advisory on the repository, or by contacting the maintainer
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments,
close issues, or block people who do not follow this code of conduct.
